The Art Nouveau Centre

The story of the fire and the rebuilding, inside an original 1907 pharmacy with its interiors intact.

Day 9 · Sun Aug 16 · 13:30–14:30An hour, indoors, and it is the piece that makes the walk outside mean something — you see the fire and the rebuild explained, and then one complete interior of the period rather than just facades. Ticket includes KUBE next door.
Housed inThe former Swan Pharmacy, 1907
ShowsOriginal Art Nouveau interiors, furniture and decorative art
Cost~NOK 130, with KUBE art museum next door
IndoorsFully — the rain-proof option for this day

The Swan Pharmacy, built in 1907 as part of the reconstruction, kept its original Art Nouveau interiors — panelling, fittings, painted decoration and furniture — and is now the national centre for Art Nouveau in Norway. It is a small museum and it does two things well.

First, the "Time Machine": a walk-through account of the night of the fire and the years of rebuilding that followed. Second, the building itself: the pharmacy hall, the dining room and the stair are preserved as designed, so you get the inside of the style you have been looking at from the street all morning.

The ticket also covers KUBE, the art museum in the former Norges Bank building next door.
