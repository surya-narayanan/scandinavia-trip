The Mostraumen fjord cruise

Three and a half hours from the middle of Bergen up a fjord that narrows to a tidal strait you can almost touch.

Day 11 · Tue Aug 18 · 10:00–13:30Your one fjord cruise from Bergen, and the only one you need. It leaves from and returns to the city centre — there is no transfer at either end, whatever other itineraries claim. The bow goes under a waterfall and ponchos are handed out; your shells are better. Confirm the 10:00 departure actually runs on Aug 18 when you book.

The turnMostraumen, a strait so narrow the boat reverses through it

The boat leaves from Zachariasbryggen beside the Fish Market, crosses Byfjorden, and heads north into Osterfjorden — away from the cruise-ship fjords and into working countryside. The fjord narrows steadily as you go, the walls closing in and the farms getting smaller, until at Mostraumen the channel is only a few dozen metres wide.

Mostraumen is a tidal strait: the water squeezing through it runs like a river and reverses with the tide, and the skipper has to time the passage. The boat noses through, turns, and comes back — and at that point the rock is close enough on both sides that the scale of the fjord finally registers.

What happens on the way

The boat pushes its bow in under a waterfall so passengers can fill a glass from it, which is a set piece but a good one. There are goats on the banks in places, seals occasionally, and several farms only reachable by water. Ponchos are provided at the waterfall; if it is raining properly, your own shell is the better option, and the outside deck is where the trip actually happens.

On Day 12 you drive back down this same fjord by road on the way home from Hardanger — the Osterfjord return leg — so you get it from both the water and the land.
