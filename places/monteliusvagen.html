Monteliusvägen

A 500-metre clifftop path on Södermalm looking straight across the water at the old city.

Day 16 · Sun Aug 23 · 18:30 onwardsMetro to Slussen and a 10-minute uphill walk with some stairs — your parents’ call. Sunset is about 20:30, and dinner in SoFo afterwards.
Length~500 m
WhereAlong the Södermalm cliff above Riddarfjärden
Looks atRiddarholmen, Gamla Stan, the City Hall
CostFree
AccessUphill from Slussen, with some stairs

Södermalm sits on a ridge, and Monteliusvägen runs along the top of its northern cliff — a narrow gravel and boardwalk path, half a kilometre long, with allotment cottages and small wooden houses on the uphill side and a drop to the water on the other.

The view is the best free one in Stockholm: Riddarfjärden directly below, Riddarholmen’s church spire, the whole northern edge of Gamla Stan, and the City Hall tower across the water. It faces roughly north-west, which is why it is a sunset spot.

It is a residential lane rather than a viewpoint with facilities — there are benches and nothing else, and it gets busy on a warm evening. The stairs up from Slussen are the only awkward part.
