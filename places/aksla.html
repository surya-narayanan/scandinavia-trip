Mount Aksla and Fjellstua

Aksla / Fjellstua

The viewpoint that explains Ålesund: the whole Art Nouveau town laid out on its islands below you.

Day 9 · Sun Aug 16 · 10:00–11:15Your plan pre-books a taxi up and explicitly skips the 418 steps with your parents, which is the right call. Walkers can take the stepped park path down while the others ride. Morning light is best from here.
Height~189 m
On foot418 steps up through Byparken
By roadTaxi or car to the top, ~NOK 200
CostFree — there is a café at the summit

Ålesund is built across several islands and a narrow neck of land, and from the ground you cannot see the shape of it. From Aksla you can: the harbour and the Brosundet canal cutting through the middle, the tight grid of towers and gables, the Sunnmøre alps behind and the Atlantic beyond. It is the stop that makes the rest of the town legible.

The climb is 418 steps up through Byparken, a genuine but unremarkable staircase, with landings and benches. The road to the top exists and taxis use it. Fjellstua at the summit is a café with a terrace and, on a clear morning, a view out to the Runde bird cliffs.
