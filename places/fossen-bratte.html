Fossen Bratte

A roadside waterfall dropping straight down beside the road in Samnanger — fifteen minutes, no walking.

Day 12 · Wed Aug 19 · 08:25–08:40The first stop of the Hardanger day, 55 minutes out of Bergen, and it is a leg-stretch as much as a sight — no walking required.
Height~90 m
WhereSamnanger, on the road east from Bergen
Time15 min stop
CostFree

Fossen Bratte — literally “the steep waterfall” — comes down about ninety metres in a single plunge a short distance from the road in Samnanger. There is a pull-in, a short path, and a view of the fall from below, and in wet weather it is loud and full.

It is a fifteen-minute stop and it functions as the day’s first break after nearly an hour on the bus. Sensible place to use the facilities and get the camera out of the bag for the first time.
