Norwegian Fjord Centre and Fossevandring

A museum about how the fjords were made and lived in, with a stepped walkway down beside a waterfall.

Day 8 · Sat Aug 15 · 09:30–11:00Take the taxi or shuttle up and walk down the 327 steps beside the falls — the railings run the whole way and your parents can set the pace. Doing it in this direction is the entire trick.
The centreNorsk Fjordsenter, on the hillside above the village
The walkFossevandring — 327 steps beside Storfossen, with railings throughout
Cost~NOK 195
DirectionGo up by taxi or shuttle, walk down

The Fjord Centre is the interpretation centre for the West Norwegian Fjords World Heritage site, and it covers the things the boat trip cannot explain: how the glaciers cut these valleys, how the cliff farms actually functioned, the rockslides and tsunamis that periodically destroyed villages, and the coming of the cruise ships.

The Fossevandring is a stepped path and boardwalk running down the hillside alongside Storfossen, the waterfall that comes through the village. It is 327 steps, fully railed, with viewing platforms out over the water and the falls loud beside you the whole way. Walking down it from the centre to the village is a genuinely good twenty minutes, and it puts you back in Geiranger for the rest of the morning.

If the weather is clear, the alternative use of the late morning is the hop-on bus up to Flydalsjuvet, the rock ledge that produces the standard postcard view down the whole fjord with a cruise ship in it.
