Bases: your Copenhagen apartment (Vesterbro, next to Central Station & Tivoli) (Aug 8–12) · your Oslo apartment (Kvadraturen, by Oslo S), Oslo (Aug 12–14 & Aug 20–21) · Geiranger / Ålesund / Bergen hotels · your Stockholm Airbnb (Östermalm, by Östermalmstorg), Stockholm (Aug 21–25).
