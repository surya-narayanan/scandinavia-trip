Scandinavia Aug 8–25, 2026 — Full Timed Schedule

Bases: your Copenhagen apartment (Vesterbro, next to Central Station & Tivoli) (Aug 8–12) · your Oslo apartment (Kvadraturen, by Oslo S), Oslo (Aug 12–14 & Aug 20–21) · Geiranger / Ålesund / Bergen hotels · your Stockholm Airbnb (Östermalm, by Östermalmstorg), Stockholm (Aug 21–25).

Row colors: PURPLE = purchased ticket, time is fixed · BLUE = transit to arrange · CREAM = meals/rest · WHITE = sights. Priorities: ★ MUST SEE · ○ worth it · ✕ SKIP. Times use typical timetables — confirm live in the Rejsekort/Vy/Skyss/SL apps on the day. Prices approximate.

Transit systems: Copenhagen = Rejsekort app (tap in/out) or ticket machines; the Copenhagen Card DISCOVER (96-hr, DKK 1,219 per adult at 2026 prices, copenhagencard.com) covers all transport + nearly every entry Aug 9–12, and saves ~DKK 350–400 per adult over paying separately. Oslo = Ruter app (24-hr pass ~NOK 132). Stockholm = just tap a contactless credit card on every SL gate/ferry (~SEK 43/ride, auto-caps at ~SEK 175/day).

COPENHAGEN SHOPPING — RANKED BY AREA, THEN BY PRICE

VERDICT: ranked by walking distance from your Vesterbro flat, then filtered against your ~$100–200 (≈DKK 700–1,400) per-item budget. That budget is the useful fact here — it clears almost every Danish brand on the list comfortably, tops out at Han Kjøbenhavn, and goes furthest of all on vintage, where the same money buys archival designer instead of a plain tee. CORRECTION to what this page said earlier: I had written that the second-hand scene needs a Nørrebro trip you don’t have time for. That was wrong — there is a four-door vintage block in Indre By, 7 minutes flat from your front door, and it is now the Day 3 plan.

SALES — WHAT YOU ARE ACTUALLY WALKING INTO: neither Denmark nor Sweden has official sale dates. Summer markdowns start late June, run 30–70% off, and are in their FINAL clearance stage by mid-August as autumn stock lands. So: SANDALS AND T-SHIRTS ARE THE SALE BUY — they are exactly the end-of-summer stock being cleared while you are there. A JACKET IS NOT — outerwear arriving in late August is new-season and full price, so buy your jacket vintage (the Day 3 block) or accept paying full. Deepest remaining markdowns sit on the clearance racks of the department stores (Magasin, Illum) rather than in the small boutiques, and at Magasin the 10% non-resident discount may stack on top of a markdown — worth asking at the till. Sizes will be picked over; go early in the day, not at 17:45.

TAX-FREE — THERE IS A TRAP IN YOUR ROUTE. As a non-EU resident you can reclaim Danish VAT, but you must get the paperwork validated when you LEAVE THE EU — and you leave the EU on Aug 12 flying CPH → Oslo, because Norway is outside the EU (Schengen, but not EU customs). So any Danish clothing refund has to be processed at CPH airport that afternoon, NOT later in Stockholm — your Day 5 window is 14:30–17:00, which is enough if you go straight to the refund desk before check-in.
